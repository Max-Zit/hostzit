Files, SFTP and SSH

Your account sees only its own home folder. Websites are in ~/sites, logs in ~/logs, backups in ~/backups.

File manager

Files lists, uploads (up to 48 MB per file), downloads, edits text files, renames, changes permissions and deletes. For many or large files use SFTP.

SFTP and SSH

Under SFTP and SSH set a separate password or add SSH keys. Connect with FileZilla, WinSCP, Cyberduck or sftp ACCOUNT@SERVER. With SSH in your package you get a shell (ssh ACCOUNT@SERVER) in your own environment: you see only your files and processes; tools like PHP, WP-CLI, git, tar and the MariaDB client are there.

Scheduled jobs

Scheduled jobs run commands on a schedule in cron syntax (*/15 * * * * every 15 minutes, 0 3 * * * daily at 3:00, @hourly). They run as your account in your own environment and with your limits; Run now starts a job at once, the output is kept in its log.

Backups

Make a backup now saves all files, all databases and the settings of the websites, zones and jobs into ~/backups/hostzit-DATE.tar.gz. It counts against your disk space; download it with SFTP and delete old ones.
