Domains and DNS

HostZit runs authoritative name servers (PowerDNS). Zones of the customers are published within seconds of a change.

Zones

A zone is created with a new website (when asked) or under Domains and DNS. New zones get A/AAAA records for the domain and www, a CAA record for Let's Encrypt and the SPF record set by the administrator. To use them, enter the server's name servers at the domain registrar.

Records

The editor accepts A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, SRV, CAA, NS (delegation of a subdomain), TLSA, HTTPS, SVCB and PTR. Names are relative to the zone: @ for the domain itself, www, _dmarc. Values are checked before they are saved. Records managed by EmailZit are marked and cannot be changed here.

DNSSEC

Sign the zone switches DNSSEC on; put the DS record shown at the registrar. Before switching it off, remove the DS record at the registrar first.

Secondary name servers

Under Settings → Hosting, enter the IP addresses of other name servers that should copy every zone (for example a second server as ns2). They may then transfer the zones (AXFR) and are notified of each change.
